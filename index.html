Our vision

Papers are one output of our research. Lasting infrastructure is the other.

Our long-term ambition is to build foundational security technologies that other researchers, developers and companies can integrate into their AI systems. AI security is our main application domain; formal methods and cryptography are our equally important scientific foundations.

Real problems drive research

Our research starts from meaningful security problems in real AI systems.

Rigorous foundations

Formal methods and cryptography give precise security definitions, analysis and verifiable guarantees.

Infrastructure creates lasting impact

Research should produce reusable, maintained technologies, not only publications.

Analyze. Verify. Prove.

Three capabilities tie our research together.

Analyze

Find security vulnerabilities, coordinated attacks and failure conditions.

Verify

Check whether systems satisfy precisely defined security properties.

Prove

Produce evidence another party can check independently, potentially without seeing sensitive information.

Research results.

Peer-reviewed papers from our group, at venues including CCS, USENIX Security, IEEE S&P, NeurIPS and OOPSLA.
